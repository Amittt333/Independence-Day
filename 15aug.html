Chronicles of Valor

The Freedom Journey

A relentless 90-year national saga of courage, sacrifice, and unyielding will that forged our sovereign Republic.

1857

The First War of Independence

Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi, Veer Kunwar Singh, and Bahadur Shah Zafar ignited the historic rebellion that shattered the East India Company's supremacy.

1919

Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

The tragedy of Baisakhi day in Amritsar awakened the conscience of the nation, prompting Rabindranath Tagore to renounce his knighthood and cementing the vow for total freedom.

1930

Dandi March & Civil Disobedience

Mahatma Gandhi walked 240 miles to the Arabian Sea to make salt, transforming a common mineral into the ultimate symbol of defiance against imperial tax monopolies.

1942

Quit India Movement ("Do or Die")

The clarion call given at Gowalia Tank Ground in Bombay mobilized every corner of the country into a non-violent wave of mass civil defiance.

1947

Tryst with Destiny: Independence

At the stroke of the midnight hour on 15 August 1947, India awoke to life and freedom. The tricolor was unfurled atop the Red Fort, ending over two centuries of foreign dominion.

1950

Adoption of the Constitution

Under the leadership of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, India enacted the world's most comprehensive and democratic Constitution, establishing a sovereign, socialist, secular Republic.

2026

India @ 80: Modern Global Powerhouse

Eight decades of freedom celebrated through technological supremacy, lunar and solar deep-space exploration, green energy leadership, and 1.4 billion united citizens.

Immortal Titans

Freedom Fighters & Architects

Tribute to the courageous visionaries who sacrificed everything for Bharat's dawn.

BS

Bhagat Singh

1907 – 1931 • Revolutionary

Fearless young revolutionary whose supreme sacrifice and intellectual vision galvanized India's youth into total mobilization.

SCB

Subhas Chandra Bose

1897 – 1945 • Netaji

Commander of the Indian National Army (INA) who led the military offensive for Indian liberation with the battle-cry "Chalo Delhi".

SVP

Sardar Patel

1875 – 1950 • Iron Man of India

The visionary statesman who integrated 565+ princely states into one united, sovereign Indian Union with diplomatic firmness.

RL

Rani Lakshmibai

1828 – 1858 • Queen of Jhansi

Epitome of female martial valour and defiance who fought on horseback in 1857, immortalized as the symbol of resistance.

MKG

Mahatma Gandhi

1869 – 1948 • Father of Nation

Pioneered Satyagraha and Ahimsa (non-violence), leading millions in mass non-cooperation and civil disobedience movements.

BRA

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

1891 – 1956 • Architect of Constitution

Scholar, jurist, and social reformer who framed India's supreme Constitution, securing universal liberty, equality, and fraternity.

SN

Sarojini Naidu

1879 – 1949 • Nightingale of India

Orator, poet, and key leader of the Salt Satyagraha who championed women's franchise and national unity.

CA

Chandrashekhar Azad

1906 – 1931 • Revolutionary Leader

Reorganized the HSRA and lived by his pledge to remain forever 'Azad' (free), never captured alive by colonial forces.
