Chronicles of Valor

The Freedom Journey

A relentless saga of courage, tribal uprisings, mass movements, and sacrifice that forged our sovereign Republic.

1857

The First War of Independence

Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi, Veer Kunwar Singh, and heroes across Chota Nagpur ignited the historic rebellion that shattered colonial rule.

1895–1900

Bhagwan Birsa Munda & The 'Ulgulan' (Jharkhand)

Dharti Aaba Birsa Munda mobilized the tribal community of Jharkhand against British exploitation, spearheading a legendary uprising for land, dignity, and self-rule.

1930

Dandi March & Civil Disobedience

Mahatma Gandhi walked 240 miles to the Arabian Sea to make salt, turning a natural mineral into the ultimate symbol of defiance against imperial tax monopolies.

1942

Quit India Movement ("Do or Die") & Hazaribagh Breakout

Loknayak Jayaprakash Narayan made his legendary Diwali night escape from Hazaribagh Central Jail to lead the nationwide underground resistance.

1947

Tryst with Destiny: Midnight Dawn

At the stroke of the midnight hour on 15 August 1947, India awoke to life and freedom as the tricolor soared atop the Red Fort.

2026

India @ 80: Modern Global Powerhouse

Eight decades of freedom celebrated through lunar south pole presence, semiconductor manufacturing, world-leading digital infrastructure, and 1.4 billion united citizens.

Jharkhand (झारखंड)

Land of Bhagwan Birsa Munda, Hazaribagh National Park & Mineral Wealth

Jharkhand is known for rich tribal heritage, waterfalls, dense sal forests, Hazaribagh plateau, and mineral richness. It holds a glorious chapter in the Indian freedom struggle from the 1857 revolt to the 1942 Quit India breakout.

Immortal Titans

Freedom Fighters & Architects

Tribute to the courageous visionaries who gave their today for our tomorrow.

BM

Birsa Munda

1875 – 1900 • Dharti Aaba (Jharkhand)

Legendary tribal leader and freedom hero who championed indigenous rights and led the Ulgulan rebellion against the British Raj.

BS

Bhagat Singh

1907 – 1931 • Revolutionary

Fearless revolutionary thinker whose supreme martyrdom energized the Indian masses into unyielding rebellion.

SCB

Subhas Chandra Bose

1897 – 1945 • Netaji

Leader of the Indian National Army (Azad Hind Fauj) who challenged the empire with the historic call "Jai Hind".

SVP

Sardar Patel

1875 – 1950 • Iron Man of India

Statesman who unified 565+ princely states into one sovereign Republic with wisdom and unwavering resolve.

RL

Rani Lakshmibai

1828 – 1858 • Queen of Jhansi

The symbol of female valour and courage who led her troops from the front in the 1857 War of Independence.

MKG

Mahatma Gandhi

1869 – 1948 • Father of Nation

Championed Ahimsa (non-violence) and Satyagraha, leading mass non-cooperation and the historic Salt March.

BRA

Dr. B.R. Ambedkar

1891 – 1956 • Chief Architect

Framed the Constitution of India, enshrining justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity for all citizens.

JPN

Jayaprakash Narayan

1902 – 1979 • Hazaribagh Hero

Escaped Hazaribagh Jail in 1942 during Quit India to organize nationwide resistance; champion of Total Revolution.
